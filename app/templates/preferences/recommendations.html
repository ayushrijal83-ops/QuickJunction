How is the preference match calculated?

Each preference you set is compared with the dish: cuisine must be the same; spice counts fully when it is the same level and half when it is one level away; a dish of exactly your dietary type counts fully, a compatible one (for example a vegan dish for a vegetarian) counts half. The percentage is the average over the preferences you set — it describes how closely the dish fits what you asked for, not a prediction that you will like it.

Strong match: 80% or more · Good match: 60–79% · Fair match: 40–59% · Low match: below 40%. Items your dietary preference excludes are never shown.
